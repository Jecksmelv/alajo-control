The problem

Ajo / esusu: people with irregular incomes save small fixed amounts on a schedule and receive a lump sum for rent, school fees, inventory. 17% of Nigerian adults saved in such groups[6]; most women who save still prefer them[8].

Digital operators replaced the notebook. But at scale, every morning the sources disagree: ledger, bank transfers, provider settlements, agent cash, customer messages.

  • Spouse pays from his account
  • “Transfer from OPay” — no reference
  • Agent logs ₦30,000, remits ₦20,000
  • Receipt / bank file submitted twice
  • Card payment settles tomorrow
  • Two Adeyemis owe the same amount

False reminders break trust · wrong matches corrupt balances · gaps put payouts at risk.

2 / 10

The gap

Existing digital savings tools

AjoMoney, AjoFlow, Ajo app, Cowrywise Circles, PiggyVest…

Record what should have happened: schedules, groups, wallets, passbooks, reminders, payouts.

Alajo Control

internal operations / finance layer

Determines what actually happened and what is safe to do next: reconcile conflicting evidence, triage exceptions, see payout risk, act with human approval.

Not a savings app. Not a chatbot. A control tower for the people who keep the ledger true.

Safe autonomy

AI may do automatically

  • Routine reminder — only after payment status is verified
  • Payment acknowledgment
  • Ask for a transfer reference or receipt
  • Privacy policy reply
  • Internal tasks, field-agent info requests

Requires a human

  • Matching an ambiguous payment
  • Changing any balance or contribution status
  • Resolving agent cash discrepancies / disputes
  • Payout changes, suspensions, fraud messages

Never

Move real money · delete evidence or audit logs · disclose another customer's data · bypass approval

283 reminders automated; 130 held for evidence; 0 agent ledger postings.

Why it matters · next step

Community savings is a trust business; the ledger is the record of that trust. Alajo Control keeps it true at scale — for about $0.007 of AI per morning.

Limits: synthetic data · simulated WhatsApp & provider feeds · heuristic name matching · no human timing test yet.
