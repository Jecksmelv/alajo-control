Alajo Control

AI operations control tower for community savings

Most digital savings products solve the digitization problem.
Alajo Control solves the truth-and-action problem that comes after.

Melvin Oshomegie · MGT 409 Final Project · Yale SOM · Oct 2026

The problem

Many Nigerian traders and households earn money in small, irregular amounts. Banks were often hard to reach, inconvenient, or poorly suited to saving small sums every day.

Before: a trusted collector (“alajo”)visits in person→collects small cash→writes it in a passbook→returns the savings later
Alajo, the startup I co-founded, moved it onlinebank / agent payments→digital ledger→customer sees savings
New scaling problembank + payment provider + field agent + what customers say they paid≠always the same record

Operations teams must reconstruct what actually happened before they can safely update balances, chase genuine missed payments or manage payouts.

It is customers’ savings: a false reminder damages trust, a wrong match corrupts someone’s balance, and unresolved gaps can affect future payouts. This problem is informed by my experience co-founding Alajo, a Nigerian digital savings startup. (The demo uses synthetic data, not Alajo customer records.)

The gap

Existing digital savings tools

AjoMoney, AjoFlow, Ajo app, Cowrywise Circles, PiggyVest…

Record what should have happened: schedules, groups, wallets, passbooks, reminders, payouts.

Alajo Control

internal operations / finance layer

Determines what actually happened and what is safe to do next: reconcile conflicting evidence, triage exceptions, see payout risk, act with human approval.

Not a savings app. Not a chatbot. A control tower for the people who keep the ledger true.

The product: Morning Control Room

Real figures from SQLite: ₦7,821,000 due today · ₦7,984,000 reconciled by rules · ₦130,500 in suspense · payout gap ₦299,692 on Fri 23 Oct.

How it works

Deterministic engine resolves 1,835 records with zero model calls · AI only for genuinely ambiguous cases · approvals enforced in code.

One case: the spouse-account payment

  1. Before: Aisha Bello's ₦20,000 shows unpaid; transfer from MUSA IBRAHIM, no reference.
  2. Evidence: her WhatsApp says she used her husband's account; 1 of 58 same-amount candidates supported; reminder held.
  3. Money Match: recommend match, confidence 0.99.
  4. Human approves → ledger posts ₦20,000 once, acknowledgment queued, audit logged.

Safe autonomy

AI may do automatically

  • Routine reminder — only after payment status is verified
  • Payment acknowledgment
  • Ask for a transfer reference or receipt
  • Privacy policy reply
  • Internal tasks, field-agent info requests

Requires a human

  • Matching an ambiguous payment
  • Changing any balance or contribution status
  • Resolving agent cash discrepancies / disputes
  • Payout changes, suspensions, fraud messages

Never

Move real money · delete evidence or audit logs · disclose another customer's data · bypass approval

283 reminders automated; 130 held for evidence; 0 agent ledger postings.

Why it matters · next step

Community savings is a trust business; the ledger is the record of that trust. Alajo Control keeps it true at scale — for about $0.004 of AI per morning.

Limits: synthetic data · simulated WhatsApp & provider feeds · heuristic name matching · no human timing test yet.
